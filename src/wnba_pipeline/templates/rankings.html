Reading these numbers

Offensive rating

Points scored per 100 possessions, computed as points ÷ possessions × 100, where possessions = FGA − OREB + TOV + 0.44 × FTA. It measures scoring efficiency, not overall team strength.

Recent form

The difference between a team's last-7 offensive rating and its season rating. Positive means the team has scored more efficiently lately than it has all year. Compare it against the league figure in the note above — form moves league-wide, not just per team.

Pace

Estimated possessions per game. High pace raises both teams' point totals without implying either is more efficient, which is why the total and the rating are read together.
