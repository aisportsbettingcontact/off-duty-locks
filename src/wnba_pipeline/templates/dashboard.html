How to read this page

Markets

Every number sits on the row of the team it belongs to. The spread shown beside a team is that team's own number; the total shows the over on the top row and the under below it; moneylines are American odds. Books are DraftKings for the public line and Circa for the sharp line.

Signals

Sharp Money — money share sits 15+ points away from ticket share. Public Heavy — 70%+ of tickets on one side. Reverse Line Move — the line moved against the ticket majority. Conflict — sharp money and the line move disagree. Model Edge — Model v0 differs from the market by more than 1.5 points of spread or 2.0 of total.

Model v0 and Edge

Model v0 projects each game from last-7 offensive rating and pace plus {{ home_court }} points of home court. It is a published formula, not a trained model, and it carries no defensive term — so it disagrees with the market often. Edge scores that disagreement from 0 to 10 and stops at 10.
